# Week 19, 2026 — May 04–10

> **Key Takeaway:** The AI-and-jobs debate hit a new pitch: Cloudflare and Coinbase both posted record numbers and cut staff in the same breath, while Jensen Huang publicly pushed back on "doomer" job-loss narratives — a coordinated PR pivot as backlash builds. Meanwhile Anthropic quietly buried the hatchet with Elon Musk (a SpaceX compute deal, of all things), OpenAI shipped a pricier default model, and a Chinese court drew a legal line around firing workers "because of AI."

---

## 📰 News

- **Greg Brockman's personal journals used against him at trial** — In week two of the Musk v. Altman trial, Brockman was cross-examined using his own diary entries, including "Financially, what will take me to $1B?" Musk's team cited the entries as proof the original OpenAI nonprofit mission was never genuine. *(May 2026)*
- **Chinese court rules AI adoption isn't grounds for cheap-tier termination** — A worker was fired under China's lower-cost dismissal category, with the employer arguing AI adoption counted as an "objective major change in business environment." The court disagreed, ruling AI adoption is a management call, not force majeure, and ordered full severance. China is pushing AI dominance while simultaneously building legal guardrails for displaced workers. *(May 2026)*
- **Jensen Huang pushes back on AI "doomer" rhetoric** — At the Milken Institute, Huang argued AI is "creating an enormous number of jobs" and warned that overhyping AI's destructive potential could turn the public against the technology entirely. *(May 2026)*
- **Cloudflare's first-ever mass layoff — 1,100 cut despite record revenue** — Quarterly revenue hit a record $639.8M (+34% YoY) while internal AI usage grew 600% in three months; the company said AI-powered staff need fewer support roles around them. *(May 2026)*
- **Coinbase cuts staff, cites AI productivity gains** — Joins the growing list of companies using AI as justification for headcount reduction during a period of strong financial performance. *(May 2026)*

## 🚀 Model / Product Launches

- **Anthropic × SpaceX compute deal** — Anthropic gained access to over 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs and 300 megawatts of new capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 facility, doubling Claude Code rate limits (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) and significantly raising API limits for Opus models. Notable given Musk's past public criticism of Dario Amodei — a common enemy (OpenAI) apparently outweighs old grudges, and raises the question of whether xAI is angling to compete on infrastructure rather than models. *(May 2026)*
- **OpenAI launches GPT-5.5 Instant as new ChatGPT default** — Replaces GPT-5.3 Instant; improves hallucination rates in law and medicine, scores 81.2 on AIME 2025 (up from 65.4), deeper context management. OpenRouter analysis found users pay 49–92% more in token costs vs. GPT-5.4 despite less verbose output — efficiency gains are being captured by OpenAI, not passed to users. *(May 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- Cloudflare posts record revenue and cuts 20% of its workforce in the same breath; Coinbase does the same. Meanwhile the CEO of the company making the chips running all of it tells Milken that AI is a net job creator. All three things may be true at once, but the messaging whiplash is the story.
- DeepSeek is in talks for its first-ever external funding round, reportedly jumping from a $20B to $45B valuation within weeks, expected to be led by a Chinese state investment vehicle — worth watching as the first outside raise for a company that has so far scaled on its own.
- Despite the layoffs and the price hikes, US unemployment has stayed relatively stable — though it may be rising more quietly than the headlines suggest.
