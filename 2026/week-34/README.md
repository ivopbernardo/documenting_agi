# Week 34, 2026 — Aug 17–23

> **Key Takeaway:** Consolidation defined the week: SpaceX officially closed its $60B acquisition of Cursor and immediately launched a GitHub competitor, Stripe moved to buy AI-model marketplace OpenRouter for $7B+, and Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate hit $65 billion — up from $9B a year earlier — reportedly eyeing a $2 trillion IPO valuation this fall. Microsoft, meanwhile, quietly admitted a chunk of its AI consumer bets hadn't worked, killing Mico, Group Chats, AI podcasts, Copilot Labs, and Deep Research in one sweep.

## 🚀 Model / Product Launches

- **SpaceX closes $60B acquisition of Cursor, launches Origin** — The deal started as a compute partnership in April and turned into a full stock buyout by June. Days after closing, SpaceX launched Origin, an early-beta Git-compatible hosting platform aimed at GitHub, syncing from GitHub and letting Cursor's cloud agents clone, branch, and push directly. *(Aug 2026)*
- **Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate hits $65B** — Up from $47B in May and $9B at the end of last year; investors expect $100–120B by year-end, with the company reportedly eyeing a $2 trillion IPO valuation this fall. *(Aug 2026)*
- **Stripe reportedly closing $7B+ deal to buy OpenRouter** — The AI model marketplace routes across 400+ models so enterprises aren't locked into a single provider; payments infrastructure buying AI-routing infrastructure underscores that tokens are becoming a currency of their own. *(Aug 2026)*
- **Microsoft merges consumer and business Copilot apps, kills underperforming features** — Group Chats, AI-generated podcasts, Copilot Labs, Deep Research, and Mico (Microsoft's AI-era answer to Clippy) are all discontinued. *(Aug 2026)*
- **OpenAI ships GPT-5.6 Sol** — $4/million input, $20/million output tokens — a 20% cut on input and a third off output versus GPT-5.5. *(Aug 2026)*
- **Google ships Gemini 3.7 Flash just three weeks after 3.6 Flash** — Meaningful coding and agentic-benchmark gains at half its predecessor's per-token cost. *(Aug 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- SpaceX buying Cursor and Stripe buying OpenRouter in the same week is a real signal of sector consolidation — infrastructure and payments companies are now acquiring AI-native developer tooling outright rather than partnering with it.
- Anthropic's watermarking rollout (announced W33) drew an immediate anti-watermark tool within days — a reminder that provenance mechanisms in AI-generated content are likely to stay an arms race rather than a solved problem.
- Microsoft killing Mico alongside several other consumer AI features is a rare public admission from a major lab/platform that not every AI product bet pans out — worth noting given how rarely that gets said out loud.
