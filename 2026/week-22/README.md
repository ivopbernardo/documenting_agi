# Week 22, 2026 — May 25–31

> **Key Takeaway:** Pope Leo XIV waded into AI politics with an encyclical on concentrated tech power, while a company reportedly burned $500 million in a single month on ungoverned Claude Code usage — a stark reminder that agentic AI without spending guardrails is a live financial risk. Meanwhile Sam Altman and Dario Amodei both quietly walked back their jobs-apocalypse rhetoric right as their companies prepare blockbuster IPOs.

---

## 📰 News

- **Company burns $500M in a month on ungoverned Claude Code usage** — An AI consultant told Axios that an unnamed enterprise client racked up the bill simply because nobody capped employee usage; engineers were reportedly running agentic workflows to check the weather, at hundreds of dollars a pop. *(May 2026)*
- **Microsoft quietly scales back internal Claude Code licences; Uber blows through its entire 2026 AI budget by April** — Both disclosed the same week as the $500M overspend story, underscoring how fast agentic-coding costs can spiral without controls. *(May 2026)*
- **Pope Leo XIV publishes encyclical "Magnifica Humanitas"** — Uses AI as a lens to diagnose concentrated power, eroding democracy, and an unaccountable tech elite; explicitly criticises "technological messianism," the belief that enough compute solves enough problems. *(May 2026)*
- **Altman and Amodei walk back jobs-apocalypse rhetoric** — Both leaders softened earlier warnings about mass AI-driven unemployment in the same week — a shift Fortune connected to both companies preparing for IPOs. Altman told an Asia-Pacific audience mass displacement is unlikely, even while acknowledging entire categories like customer service are disappearing. *(May 2026)*

## 🚀 Model / Product Launches

- **Anthropic launches Claude Opus 4.8** — Upgrades coding, agentic, and reasoning benchmarks; Fast mode runs 2.5x faster at a third of the cost of previous models. Launched the same week Anthropic closed its Series H, becoming the most valued startup in the world. *(May 2026)*
- **Asana acquires Stack AI (~$75M)** — Adds no-code AI agent-building to its project management platform; Asana's Q1 revenue hit $205M even as its stock sits 53% below its January peak. *(May 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- The Pope's encyclical isn't really about AI — it's about who controls the future and whether anyone is accountable for that. Whether it's a genuine concern about concentrated power or a competing institution eyeing a new rival in the "meaning" business is a fair question.
- DuckDuckGo saw nearly 28% more visits the week after Google touted how much users "love" AI Mode — a signal that at least some users want a choice rather than forced AI integration.
- Altman and Amodei softening their jobs-apocalypse messaging right before their companies' IPOs is unlikely to be coincidental — "mass unemployment incoming" is a much harder pitch to retail investors than to policymakers.
