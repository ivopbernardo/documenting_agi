# Week 32, 2026 — Aug 03–09

> **Key Takeaway:** A DeepMind exodus dominated the week — Demis Hassabis stepped back from CEO to chairman/chief scientist, and Jeff Dean (Google's 30th employee, 27-year veteran) left to found a research-automation startup alongside three other DeepMind veterans, the same week Google lost Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer to OpenAI and Nobel laureate John Jumper to Anthropic. DeepSeek, meanwhile, signaled the "everything for pennies" era of AI pricing might be ending.

---

## 📰 News

- **Demis Hassabis steps back from DeepMind CEO to chairman + Alphabet chief scientist** — Shifting focus toward AGI strategy and Isomorphic Labs, which he calls AI's best current use case; Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over daily DeepMind operations, reporting directly to Sundar Pichai. Hassabis says AGI is close and getting the next steps right matters more than his title. Alphabet shares dropped 5% on the news. *(Aug 2026)*
- **Jeff Dean leaves Google to found Discovery Loop** — Alongside fellow DeepMind veterans Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, aiming to run thousands of parallel experiments to automate scientific and ML research, including using AI to improve AI. Lands the same week as Hassabis's reshuffle and days after Google lost Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer to OpenAI and Nobel laureate John Jumper to Anthropic. *(Aug 2026)*
- **DeepSeek signals first-ever price increase** — Breaking from the aggressive discounting that made V4 Flash a threat to Western labs; DeepSeek currently charges $0.14/$0.28 per million input/output tokens against Anthropic's $10/$50 for Fable 5, and has told users to brace for a "significant" hike without specifying numbers. *(Aug 2026)*

## 📄 Research Papers

- **Mistral releases Shieldstral** — A 3-billion-parameter open-weights safety classifier that matches guardrail models seven times its size. Takes a policy as a plain-language question at inference time ("does this image show something unsafe for a minor?") and returns a calibrated yes/no score with no retraining required, covering both text and images through the same interface. *(Aug 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- Anthropic is assembling a custom silicon team to co-design chips alongside its models, joining OpenAI (Broadcom-built Jalapeño), Google, and Meta in running proprietary silicon — with reported talks with Samsung on manufacturing, on top of existing compute deals with AWS, Google, NVIDIA, and AMD.
- Three of DeepMind's best people walking out the same week its CEO stepped back from day-to-day running rarely explains itself in a single press release — expect more of the real story to surface over the coming months. As Dean himself put it: "Operating outside a public company gives the team room to make calls that aren't necessarily in the company's purest financial interests."
- A quieter week overall (likely northern-hemisphere summer), but the underlying pace of change shows no sign of slowing.
