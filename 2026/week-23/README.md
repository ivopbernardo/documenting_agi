# Week 23, 2026 — Jun 01–07

> **Key Takeaway:** Anthropic filed for what could be one of the largest IPOs in history at a near-trillion-dollar valuation, Florida became the first US state to sue OpenAI over child safety, and Meta's AI support chatbot was tricked into handing hackers the keys to Instagram accounts — no identity verification required. Uber, having blown its entire 2026 AI budget by April, capped employee agentic-tool spending at $1,500/month per tool.

---

## 📰 News

- **Florida sues OpenAI and Sam Altman** — First-of-its-kind, 83-page state complaint alleging OpenAI marketed ChatGPT to children while concealing known risks and prioritising the AI race over user wellbeing. *(Jun 2026)*
- **Meta's AI support chatbot hijacked to steal Instagram accounts** — Hackers successfully changed account email addresses simply by asking Meta's AI chatbot, with no identity verification required; Meta says the flaw is fixed, but reports suggest attacks continued after the announcement. *(Jun 2026)*
- **Trump signs scaled-back AI executive order** — Establishes a voluntary framework for AI companies to submit frontier models for government review up to 30 days before release, plus AI cybersecurity benchmarks and a clearinghouse; a broader mandatory version had been shelved in May after industry pushback. *(Jun 2026)*
- **GitLab cuts ~350 jobs (14% of staff), exits 22 countries** — Redirecting savings into "GitLab Act 2," a rebuild for 100x growth in agentic AI workloads; reported Q1 FY2027 revenue of $264M, up 23% YoY — another record-revenue-plus-layoffs data point. *(Jun 2026)*

---

## 🚀 Model / Product Launches

- **Anthropic files draft S-1 with the SEC** — Sets up an IPO that could land as early as October and raise up to $60B, at a valuation just under $1 trillion; days later tapped Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan Chase as lead underwriters. Timed alongside SpaceX's own planned June listing and OpenAI's IPO ambitions — whoever moves first sets the benchmark. *(Jun 2026)*
- **NVIDIA Vera CPU enters full production; RTX Spark announced at Computex 2026** — Vera's early adopters include Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX, Oracle, and ByteDance. RTX Spark is a new Arm-based Windows superchip combining a Grace CPU and Blackwell GPU (up to 1 petaflop, 128GB unified memory), with Dell, HP, Microsoft, Lenovo, ASUS, and MSI building systems around it — NVIDIA explicitly moving from training dominance into inference, agents, and personal computing. *(Jun 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- Anthropic heading to public markets at nearly a trillion dollars, a state government filing an 83-page child-safety lawsuit against OpenAI, hackers using Meta's own AI support bot against it, and Uber capping employee AI spend — all in one week is a fair snapshot of how fast the ecosystem's risk surface is expanding alongside its valuations.
- Brian Chesky reportedly planning to back his own AI lab while remaining Airbnb CEO fits a pattern of operator-turned-founder bets emerging from inside large incumbents rather than pure startups.
- IPO roadshow scheduling (Anthropic, SpaceX, OpenAI all circling public markets around the same window) will set the valuation tone for the AI sector for the next year.
