# Week 27, 2026 — Jun 29–Jul 05

> **Key Takeaway:** The US government considering an equity stake in frontier AI labs went from fringe idea to floated proposal, with OpenAI reportedly offering 5% of itself to a sovereign wealth fund to smooth relations with the administration. Meanwhile Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5, a Chinese open-weight model (GLM-5.2) closed in on the frontier, and every major cloud vendor discovered that enterprise AI deployment is its own multi-billion-dollar services business.

---

## 📰 News

- **OpenAI floats handing 5% equity to a US sovereign wealth fund** — Reportedly proposed by Sam Altman to smooth relations with the administration, with other AI companies expected to contribute similar stakes. *(Jul 2026)*
- **US lifts export licence requirements on Mythos and Fable** — Access started returning July 1, framed around security commitments largely already pledged voluntarily months earlier — reinforcing the read that the original ban functioned as leverage rather than a genuine safety measure. *(Jul 2026)*

---

## 🚀 Model / Product Launches

- **Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5** — Its most agentic Sonnet yet: plans, drives browsers and terminals, and runs autonomously at a level that previously required a much larger, pricier model. Gets close to Opus 4.8 on reasoning, coding, and tool use, with an adjustable effort dial and introductory pricing of $2/million input tokens through August. *(Jul 2026)*
- **Base44 ships Base1, its own proprietary vibe-coding model** — Wix-owned platform trained a model on tens of millions of real user builds rather than renting frontier models, betting that owning the model is the only path to structurally better margins as usage scales. *(Jul 2026)*
- **Microsoft launches Microsoft Frontier** — A $2.5B, 6,000-engineer deployment business focused purely on getting enterprise AI to work in production. AWS committed $1B to the same idea two days earlier; OpenAI's equivalent has raised $10B, Anthropic's $1.5B — every major vendor now running a forward-deployed-engineer model. *(Jul 2026)*
- **Mirendil raises $200M at a $1B valuation, pre-product** — Founded by two Anthropic alumni; building AI that designs experiments, tunes settings, evaluates models, and runs training rounds — selling the "research automation" engine Anthropic uses internally (where Claude reportedly writes 80%+ of its own code) to outside labs. *(Jul 2026)*

## 💻 Repos & Code

- **Z.ai GLM-5.2 (open weights, MIT licence)** — Chinese lab's open flagship built for long-horizon work: a genuinely usable 1M-token context plus an adjustable effort dial (the same idea behind Sonnet 5). Scores 81.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 against Opus 4.8's 85.0, edges out GPT-5.5 on FrontierSWE, and sits ahead of Gemini 3.1 Pro — currently the strongest open model available. *(Jul 2026)*

## 💬 Opinion & Analysis

- Microsoft's Frontier launch is another validation of the consulting-plus-product model: software doesn't deploy itself, and at $2.5B a pop the largest companies on earth are rediscovering that someone has to sit next to the customer and make it work.
- On the geopolitical side, the US government considering equity stakes in AI labs while China pulls ahead in open source is a genuinely new axis of competition — not just capability, but ownership structure.
- The technological exponential shows no sign of slowing: new proprietary models, new deployment armies, and new research-automation moonshots, all inside a single week.
